ossia (PRISM) for Unity
=======================

Exposes the objects of a Unity scene, and the fields of your own scripts,
over OSCQuery, for Max (ossia-prism), TouchDesigner or any OSCQuery client.

The documentation is README.md, next to this file: installation, the
samples "Custom parameters" and "Exposed cube", declaring your own
parameters, reference, limits and troubleshooting.
