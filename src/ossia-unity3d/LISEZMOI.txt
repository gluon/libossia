ossia (PRISM) for Unity 2.1.0+prism.12
======================================

Exposes the objects of a Unity scene, and the fields of your own scripts,
over OSCQuery, for Max (ossia-prism), TouchDesigner or any OSCQuery client;
follows a remote OSCQuery device with Ossia.Client and Ossia.Remote.

The documentation is README.md, next to this file: installation, the
samples "Custom parameters", "Exposed cube" and "Follow a device",
declaring your own parameters, following a device, reference, limits and
troubleshooting.
